Key insights (full dataset)

  • Overall attrition is 16.1% (237 of 1,470).
  • Overtime workers leave at 30.5% vs 10.4%; single employees working overtime reach 49.6%.
  • Sales Representatives (39.8%) and Lab Technicians (23.9%) have the highest role-level attrition; both are among the lowest-paid roles.
  • Leavers earned about $4,787/month vs $6,833 for stayers; employees aged 18-25 leave at 35.8%.
  • Stock option level 0 leaves at 24.4% vs 7-9% for levels 1-2.

Ethics and fairness check

  • Gender: attrition is 14.8% (F) vs 17.0% (M); within each job level, average pay differs by under 3%, so no clear gender pay gap here.
  • Privacy: show aggregates only; never expose individuals or very small groups (e.g. Research Director).
  • Misuse risk: these patterns describe groups, not individuals. Using them to score or target specific employees (flagging "likely leavers", denying promotion) would be unfair and unreliable.
  • Data limits: synthetic, imbalanced (84% stayers), and correlation is not causation. Overtime may signal workload rather than cause leaving.
  • Recommended use: fix systemic drivers (overtime load, pay in low-paid roles, early-career support) rather than monitor individuals.
